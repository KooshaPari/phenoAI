# Eidolon Brand Identity

## Overview
**Eidolon** — A framework for building phantoms: invisible, responsive agents that work behind the scenes.

**Tagline:** Invisible presence, visible impact.

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Accent | #4338CA | Primary interactive elements, CTAs, highlights |
| Neutral | #6B7280 | Body text, secondary UI, backgrounds |

## Typography

- **Monospace** (code, technical UI): IBM Plex Mono, Roboto Mono, or system monospace
- **Body** (prose, labels): Inter, Helvetica Neue, or system sans-serif

## Visual Identity

**Logo Asset:** `assets/logo.svg` (256x256 SVG, uppercase "E" in deep indigo)

**Symbol Rationale:** The letter "E" represents *essence* and *ephemeral*—Eidolon builds on the myth of spectral presence. The deep indigo (#4338CA) conveys intelligence, mystery, and quiet sophistication. It signals that this framework operates in the conceptual, agent-driven layer of your system.

## Brand Voice

Eidolon's voice is thoughtful and exploratory. We emphasize the agent abstraction and the power of autonomous, invisible systems that perform work without explicit user choreography. Copy is precise but inviting, helping developers see their agents as first-class citizens in system architecture rather than an afterthought.
